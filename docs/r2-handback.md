## Open question and limits

**Strain is parked.** The app source at c809679c explicitly documents deviations
from the required Python oracle: intensity-once least squares versus squared
intensity weights; a robust/MAD reference versus plain component medians; and
cluster-consensus automatic basis selection versus histogram maxima. No strain
fixture or implementation is claimed. Question: should CuboStrain follow Python
exactly, replacing those app algorithms, or expose the app variants separately
with explicit deviation definitions and their own oracles? No approval is implied.

Ptychography currently supports preprocessed amplitudes, fixed positions,
full-batch complex objects, selected probe aberrations (defocus, C12, C30), probe
updates and threshold/pure-phase constraints. Additional aberrations, preprocessing,
probe centering/support constraints, position refinement and other algorithms are
not implemented. Float32 versus Double roundoff is amplified near Fourier zeros
or small local normalization: at normalization 0 and step 0.35 the diagnostic gap
was 4.781e-6, outside the fixture tolerance; endpoint fixture steps are 0.1. Exact
Fourier zeros have undefined projected phase; the pinned initial object is a fixed
weak random phase. These fixture bounds are not general error guarantees.
